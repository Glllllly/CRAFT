Narrative:
This Data Management Plan outlines the research project "Neural Plasticity in Aging Populations" (Ref: NIH-2024-8892), led by Principal Investigator Dr. Elena Vance at the Department of Neuroscience. The 36-month study, funded by the National Institutes of Health with a grant of $450,000, is currently on version 1.0, dated May 15, 2024. 

The project involves collecting high-resolution fMRI scans, cognitive assessments, and surveys, totaling approximately 2.5 TB of data in NIfTI, CSV, and JSON formats. Data collection will be handled by postdoctoral associates and clinical technicians using standardized MRI protocols and automated validation. We will utilize the BIDS metadata standard and maintain documentation through lab notebooks and codebooks, with a strict hierarchical folder structure.

Ethical approval was granted on April 10, 2024 (Ref: IRB-2024-0042). Since the study involves personal data, we will ensure GDPR compliance through pseudonymization. IP rights are held by the university. Primary data storage is on a secure university server with daily backups to AWS GovCloud, secured by AES-256 encryption and restricted access.

We intend to share the data via OpenNeuro under a CC-BY-NC 4.0 license after a 12-month embargo, though raw structural scans with identifiable facial features will remain private. Data will be retained for 10 years before being destroyed via NIST-standard digital wiping. The plan is signed by Dr. Vance, Research Integrity Officer Marcus Thorne, and Data Protection Officer Sarah Jenkins, with dates ranging from May 16 to May 19, 2024.
