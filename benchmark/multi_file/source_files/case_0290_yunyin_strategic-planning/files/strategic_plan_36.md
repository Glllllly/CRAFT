# Strategic Plan 2024-2026

## Vision
To become the industry leader in customer engagement and marketing effectiveness.

## Mission
To empower organizations with data-driven marketing solutions that drive growth and profitability.

## Core Values
- Customer Focus
- Innovation
- Excellence
- Integrity
- Collaboration

## Strategic Objectives

### 1. Market Expansion
- **Target**: 50 new customers annually
- **Approach**: Partner network, direct sales, content marketing
- **Timeline**: 2024-2026

### 2. Product Innovation
- **Target**: 3 major feature releases per year
- **Approach**: Customer feedback, market research, R&D
- **Timeline**: Quarterly releases

### 3. Customer Success
- **Target**: 95% retention rate
- **Approach**: Dedicated support, training, community
- **Timeline**: Ongoing

### 4. Team Development
- **Target**: Build high-performing teams
- **Approach**: Hiring, training, culture building
- **Timeline**: Continuous

## Financial Targets
- **Revenue 2024**: $10M
- **Revenue 2025**: $15M
- **Revenue 2026**: $25M
- **Profitability**: Break-even by Q4 2024

## Key Initiatives

### Initiative 1: Product Roadmap

### Initiative 2: Market Expansion
- Expand to Asia Pacific region

### Initiative 3: Customer Success

## Success Metrics
- Customer acquisition rate
- Customer lifetime value
- Net revenue retention
- Employee satisfaction
- Brand awareness

## Risk Management

- Regulatory changes
- Economic downturn
