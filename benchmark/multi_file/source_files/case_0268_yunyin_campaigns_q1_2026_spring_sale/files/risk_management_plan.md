# Q1 2026 Spring Sale Risk Management Plan

## Risk Identification
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|------------|--------|---------------------|
| Low campaign performance | Medium | High | Diversify channels, conduct A/B testing |
| Technical issues (website crash) | Low | Critical | Implement auto-scaling, conduct load testing |
| Supply chain delays | Medium | Medium | Order inventory in advance, have backup suppliers |
| Negative social media feedback | High | Medium | Monitor social media, prepare response templates |

## Contingency Plans
1. **Low Performance**: Reallocate budget to top-performing channels within 24 hours.
2. **Technical Issues**: Activate backup servers and notify users via email/SMS.
3. **Supply Chain Delays**: Offer alternative products or extend the campaign.
