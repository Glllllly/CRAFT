# Q1 2026 Spring Sale Post-Campaign Analysis

## Campaign Overview
- Duration: 2026-02-15 to 2026-03-31
- Budget: ¥250,000
- Target: 15k new users, ¥500k GMV

## Performance Summary
| Metric | Target | Actual | Achievement |
|--------|--------|--------|-------------|
| New Users |15,000 |18,200 |121.3% |
| GMV |¥500,000 |¥620,000 |124% |
| Conversion Rate |3.0% |3.5% |116.7% |
| ROI |120% |148% |123.3% |

## Key Learnings
1. **Channel Effectiveness**: Douyin and小红书 channels delivered highest ROI (150%+), while线下快闪 underperformed.
2. **Creative Impact**: Short-form videos with user testimonials had 2x higher CTR than product demos.
3. **Timing**: Weekend promotions (Saturday/Sunday) generated 30% more sales than weekdays.

## Recommendations
1. Allocate 60% of future campaign budget to Douyin and小红书.
2. Prioritize user testimonial videos in creative strategy.
3. Focus promotions on weekends and peak hours (18:00-22:00).

## Next Steps
- Share learnings with marketing team for Q2 campaign planning.
- Update campaign SOP to include channel performance benchmarks.
- Archive campaign assets and reports for future reference.
